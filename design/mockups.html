xSpeedTest window mockups

Fifty animated concepts for a Qt 6 / QML front-end to speedtest-cli. Each card loops the full test: server search, download, upload. Drag the Blur and Opacity sliders to see how KWin blur and Kvantum translucency change each design, then pick your favourites and send me the numbers.

Glass

Blur and tint here are CSS stand-ins. The real app asks KWin for blur behind the window and reads its colors, radius and fonts from the active Qt style and Kvantum theme, so each mockup is a layout and motion study, not a fixed palette. Speeds are simulated.
